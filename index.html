If a button doesn't work

  1. Google Calendar: on a computer, open calendar.google.com → Settings → Add calendar → From URL, paste the link. It then appears on your phone. (The phone app can't add links.)
  2. iPhone: Settings → Calendar → Accounts → Add Account → Other → Add Subscribed Calendar, paste the link.
  3. Samsung Calendar: add it in Google Calendar as above, and let it sync.

It is a separate calendar: it doesn't change your events, and you can hide it or remove it any time. Tip: turn off your app's own “week numbers” (those use the ISO rule and can differ from EW).

How EW is counted

Weeks run Sunday to Saturday. EW1 is the week that contains 4 January (the first week with at least 4 days in the new year). Days before it belong to the last week of the previous year, so a year has 52 or 53 weeks.
